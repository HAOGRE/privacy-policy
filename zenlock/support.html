常见问题

为什么 App 无法屏蔽应用？

ZenLock 需要"屏幕使用时间"权限才能正常工作。请前往设置 → 屏幕使用时间，确认已授权 ZenLock 使用家庭控制功能。

专注开始后无法提前退出？

这是 ZenLock 的核心设计——专注期间退出需要额外购买解锁，以帮助你抵御干扰。你可以在 session 开始前，通过右上角皇冠按钮提前购买单次解锁备用。

购买了"单次解锁"但没有生效？

单次解锁在购买成功后会立即结束当前专注 session。如果没有反应，请检查网络连接后重试，或联系我们。

如何恢复已购买的"终身解锁"？

打开 ZenLock，在专注页面点击右上角皇冠按钮，滚动到底部点击"恢复购买"即可。

ZenLock 会上传我的数据吗？

不会。ZenLock 完全在本地运行，不连接任何服务器，不收集任何用户数据。

灵动岛 / 锁屏倒计时没有显示？

请确认设备支持实时活动（iOS 16.2+），并在设置 → 通知 → ZenLock 中开启"实时活动"权限。

FAQ

Why isn't ZenLock blocking apps?

ZenLock requires Screen Time permission to function. Go to Settings → Screen Time and make sure ZenLock is authorized to use Family Controls.

I can't exit a focus session early?

This is by design — exiting early requires an additional purchase to help you resist distractions. You can buy a one-time unlock in advance before starting a session via the crown button in the top-right corner.

I purchased "One-Time Unlock" but nothing happened?

A one-time unlock immediately ends the current focus session upon successful purchase. If nothing happened, please check your network connection and try again, or contact us.

How do I restore my "Lifetime Unlock" purchase?

Open ZenLock, tap the crown button in the top-right corner during a focus session, scroll to the bottom, and tap "Restore Purchases".

Does ZenLock upload my data?

No. ZenLock runs entirely on-device, connects to no servers, and collects no user data.

Dynamic Island / lock screen countdown isn't showing?

Make sure your device supports Live Activities (iOS 16.2+) and that Live Activities are enabled in Settings → Notifications → ZenLock.

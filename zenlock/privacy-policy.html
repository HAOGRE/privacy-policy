隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 5 月 5 日

感谢您使用 ZenLock。本隐私政策说明我们如何（或不如何）处理您的个人信息。

我们不收集任何数据

ZenLock 不会收集、存储、传输或共享任何用户个人数据。应用的所有功能均在您的设备本地运行，不与任何服务器通信。

设备权限

ZenLock 使用 Apple 的"屏幕使用时间"（Screen Time / Family Controls）框架来提供专注锁屏功能。相关数据由 Apple 系统在本地管理，开发者无法访问。

App 内购买

ZenLock 提供 App 内购买项目（单次解锁、终身解锁）。所有购买均通过 Apple App Store 处理，我们不收集、存储或处理任何支付信息。购买记录由 Apple 管理，受 Apple 隐私政策约束。

第三方服务

ZenLock 不集成任何第三方分析、广告或追踪服务。

儿童隐私

我们不收集任何用户数据，包括儿童数据，符合《儿童在线隐私保护法》（COPPA）的相关要求。

隐私政策变更

如本政策有重大变更，我们将更新本页面顶部的日期。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 5, 2026

Thank you for using ZenLock. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle (or do not handle) your personal information.

We Do Not Collect Any Data

ZenLock does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal user data. All app functionality runs locally on your device and does not communicate with any server.

Device Permissions

ZenLock uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework to provide focus-locking features. All related data is managed locally by the Apple system and is not accessible to the developer.

In-App Purchases

ZenLock offers in-app purchases (one-time unlock, lifetime unlock). All purchases are processed through the Apple App Store. We do not collect, store, or process any payment information. Purchase records are managed by Apple and subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

ZenLock does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Children's Privacy

We do not collect any user data, including data from children, in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
